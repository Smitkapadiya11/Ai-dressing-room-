Pro Tips for Closing
TIP #1
Always personalize — mention their brand name, city, and product category in every message.
TIP #2
Use FOMO — "Only X slots left", "Your competitor already started their Diwali campaign".
TIP #3
Offer free value first — free audit, free strategy session, competitor analysis.
TIP #4
Follow up 3x minimum — most deals close on the 3rd to 5th touchpoint.
TIP #5
Share screen recordings showing their competitor's ads running — strongest proof.
TIP #6
Start with Surat — it's your biggest market and has the highest concentration of potential clients.
