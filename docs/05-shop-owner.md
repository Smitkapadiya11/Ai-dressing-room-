# PROMPT 05 — The shop owner's side
Three web screens. This is what makes a shop still pay you in month nine, so it
deserves real design, not a bootstrap admin panel.

---

Create three connected desktop web screens for the shop owner's side of a
premium AI clothing try-on product called KAPADIYA & SONS. The customer-facing
kiosk is dark and cinematic; this side is the opposite — light, clear, calm,
built to be read quickly on a tablet behind a shop counter.

SCREEN 1 — "Today"
The dashboard a shop owner opens each morning. Top bar with the KAPADIYA & SONS
wordmark on the left in #1A1D22, and on the right the shop's name "Shree Saree
Sangam · Ring Road" with a small avatar.
A row of four large stat cards: "48" with the label "try-ons today", "312" with
"this month", "Red Banarasi Silk" with "most tried today", "6–8 pm" with
"busiest hour". Numbers in Playfair Display 44px, labels in Manrope 13px grey.
Below, a wide card with a simple line chart of try-ons per day over the last 30
days, one thin champagne line on a light grid.
Beside it, a narrower card titled "Tried but not sold" listing five garment
thumbnails with counts — the list a shop owner will actually act on.

SCREEN 2 — "Collection"
The catalogue manager. A large drag-and-drop area at the top with a dashed
border reading "Drop garment photos here". Below it a grid of garment cards,
each showing a 3:4 photo, the garment name, price, available sizes as small
chips, and a toggle for "showing on the mirror". One card is mid-upload,
showing a thin champagne progress line across its bottom. A search field and a
category filter row sit above the grid.

SCREEN 3 — "Your month"
The monthly report, designed to be read and then forwarded. A clean document
layout, not a dashboard: a headline in Playfair Display 40px reading "September
at Shree Saree Sangam", then four large figures with one-line explanations
beneath each, then a ranked list of the ten most-tried garments with small
thumbnails, then a short plain-English paragraph of observations. A "Send to
WhatsApp" button in the top right.

Style for all three: background #FAFAF8, cards white with 16px rounded corners,
a 1px #E8E4DC border and a very soft shadow. Text #1A1D22, secondary #6B7280.
Accent #A8853F used sparingly — only on the chart line, active toggles and the
primary button. Headings Playfair Display, everything else Manrope. Generous
white space, a clear 12-column grid, nothing crowded. No coloured icon circles,
no accent stripes on cards, no gradients.

Platform: desktop web, 1440px wide.
