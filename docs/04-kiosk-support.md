# PROMPT 04 — Consent, look book, size advice, and the failure states
The screens nobody demos and every real installation needs.

---

Create four connected mobile screens, 9:16 portrait, continuing a premium AI
clothing try-on mirror for a luxury saree showroom in India. Dark and calm.

SCREEN 1 — "Consent"
Shown once before the camera turns on. Background #08090B, no photograph.
Centred vertically with generous space. At the top, the wordmark KAPADIYA &
SONS in #C9A961 13px uppercase letter spacing 0.3em. In the middle, in Playfair
Display 40px #F5F3EF: "May we use the camera?" Below in Manrope 17px #F5F3EF,
three short lines each with a small champagne tick to the left:
"Your photo is deleted within 60 seconds",
"No face recognition, ever",
"Only you decide what goes to your phone".
Below that, two pill buttons side by side: a solid champagne one reading "YES,
BEGIN" and a ghost outlined one reading "NOT NOW". At the very bottom, in 11px
#8A9099, three language options — "ગુજરાતી · हिन्दी · English" — with Gujarati
underlined as the current one.

SCREEN 2 — "Look Book"
A single tall composition designed to be sent to a phone. At the top, the
wordmark KAPADIYA & SONS in champagne, and beneath it in Playfair Display 34px
#F5F3EF: "Your looks today". Below, a vertical stack of four photographs of the
same woman in four outfits, each 3:4, 20px rounded corners, with the garment
name and price beside it in Manrope. At the bottom, a white QR card on the left
and, on the right, the shop's address and phone in 12px #8A9099. The whole
thing should look like a page from a fashion magazine, not an app screen.

SCREEN 3 — "Size advice"
A full-bleed photograph of the woman wearing the outfit. Floating over the
lower third, a dark frosted card with 20px rounded corners containing: in
Playfair Display 30px #F5F3EF, "This fits you in M". Under it in Manrope 14px
#8A9099: "Based on your shoulder and height". Under that, a row of five size
chips — XS, S, M, L, XL — where M is filled champagne with dark text and the
others are outlined in #23262C. At the bottom of the card, a ghost pill button
reading "ASK STAFF TO BRING IT".

SCREEN 4 — "Something went wrong"
Background #08090B, no photograph. Centred: in Playfair Display 36px #F5F3EF,
"That one didn't come out". Below in Manrope 16px #8A9099, one line of plain
explanation: "The connection dropped while we were working. Nothing was saved."
Below that, a solid champagne pill button reading "TRY AGAIN" and beneath it a
plain text link in #8A9099 reading "Back to the mirror". The screen should feel
calm and designed, never like an error — no red, no warning icon, no alert
triangle.

Style for all four: background #08090B, surfaces #14161A, accent #C9A961, text
#F5F3EF, secondary #8A9099. Playfair Display for headings, Manrope for
everything else. Very generous spacing. No coloured icon circles, no stripes,
no gradients.

Platform: mobile, 9:16 portrait, 1080 by 1920.
