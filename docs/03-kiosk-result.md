# PROMPT 03 — Processing, the reveal, and compare
The eight seconds of waiting and the moment that sells the product.

---

Create three connected mobile screens, 9:16 portrait, continuing a premium AI
clothing try-on mirror for a luxury saree showroom. Dark, calm, expensive.

SCREEN 1 — "Working"
The camera view is still behind, now darkened heavily and blurred. Dead centre,
floating: the selected garment's thumbnail, 3:4 vertical, 240px tall, 20px
rounded corners, with a faint champagne ring around it suggesting a slow pulse.
Under it, 40px down: the garment name "Red Banarasi Silk" in Playfair Display
26px #F5F3EF. Under that a very thin progress line, 300px wide, 2px tall, about
70% filled in champagne, the unfilled part #23262C. Under that, 24px down, in
Manrope 11px uppercase letter spacing 0.26em in #8A9099: "RENDERING THE FABRIC".
No spinner. No percentage number. Nothing else on the screen.

SCREEN 2 — "The Result"
A full-bleed photograph of the same woman now wearing the red saree, filling the
entire screen edge to edge, with a soft dark vignette at the top and bottom.
Top bar: the KAPADIYA & SONS wordmark on the left in #C9A961, and on the right
"Your look" in #E8D5A8 11px uppercase.
On the right side, vertically centred, floating over the photograph: a white
rounded card 150px wide containing a QR code, with "SCAN TO KEEP IT" beneath it
in 10px uppercase dark grey. Below that card, a pill button with a 1px champagne
outline and a small left arrow, reading "BACK TO MIRROR" in 11px uppercase.
Along the bottom edge: the garment name in Playfair Display 24px #F5F3EF on the
left, and on the right in Manrope 13px #8A9099 the price "₹12,400" and below it
in smaller text "Available in S, M, L".
A single hairline champagne progress bar, 2px, runs along the very bottom edge
of the screen, about two-thirds full, showing time remaining before it resets.

SCREEN 3 — "Compare"
A two-by-two grid of four full-length photographs of the same woman in four
different outfits, filling the screen with only 8px gaps between them. Each has
the garment name and price in small white Manrope text at its bottom-left, over
a soft dark gradient so it stays readable. One of the four has a 2px champagne
border, showing it is selected. At the very bottom, a solid champagne pill
button in #C9A961 with #08090B text reading "MAKE MY LOOK BOOK". At the top,
small and centred, "FOUR LOOKS" in 11px uppercase #8A9099.

Style for all three: background #08090B. Playfair Display for display type,
Manrope for everything else. Accent #C9A961, text #F5F3EF, secondary #8A9099.
Photographs are the hero — let them fill the frame. Lots of negative space in
the interface elements. No coloured icon circles, no accent stripes, no
gradient buttons.

Platform: mobile, 9:16 portrait, 1080 by 1920.
