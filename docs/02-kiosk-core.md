# PROMPT 02 — The three screens that are the product
The attract screen, the live mirror, and the countdown. These are what a
customer actually sees. Paste as one prompt; Stitch handles 3 related screens
well.

---

Create three connected mobile screens, 9:16 portrait, for a premium AI clothing
try-on mirror that stands at the door of a luxury saree showroom in Surat.

SCREEN 1 — "Attract"
The idle state, running when nobody is standing there. A full-bleed dark
photographic background of a softly lit boutique interior, heavily darkened.
Centred, in Playfair Display 64px in #F5F3EF: "See it on you." Below it in
Manrope 18px #8A9099: "Step closer to begin." At the very top, small and
restrained: the wordmark KAPADIYA & SONS in Manrope 13px uppercase, letter
spacing 0.3em, in #C9A961, with a 5px champagne dot before it. Bottom of the
screen: a single thin champagne line, 60px wide, centred. Nothing else. No
buttons. The screen should look like an advertisement, not an interface.

SCREEN 2 — "The Mirror"
The main screen. The top 72% is a live camera view of a woman standing, shown
full-bleed. Over it, very faint at 20% opacity, a thin outline drawing of a
standing human figure with arms held slightly away from the body, centred, as a
positioning guide. Under the figure, in Manrope 12px uppercase letter spacing
0.26em in #F5F3EF: "STAND HERE · ARMS RELAXED AT YOUR SIDES".
Top bar over the video: wordmark KAPADIYA & SONS on the left in #C9A961 13px;
on the right the words "Mirror ready" in #E8D5A8 11px uppercase and the time.
Bottom 28%: a horizontally scrolling row of garment thumbnails, 3:4 vertical,
16px rounded, each with the garment name beneath in 13px Manrope #8A9099. Six
visible, the seventh cut off at the edge to show it scrolls. Above the row, a
small label "TODAY'S COLLECTION" in 11px uppercase #8A9099. The bottom section
sits on a dark frosted panel that blurs the video behind it.

SCREEN 3 — "Countdown"
Same live camera background, now dimmed. Dead centre: an enormous numeral "3"
in Playfair Display 180px, thin weight, in #F5F3EF, with a thin champagne
circle ring drawn around it, 300px across, the ring drawn only three-quarters
of the way round. Below the numeral, 60px down, in Manrope 13px uppercase
letter spacing 0.3em in #F5F3EF: "HOLD STILL". The garment row at the bottom is
still visible but faded to 30% opacity. Nothing else on screen.

Style for all three: background #08090B. Display type Playfair Display, all
interface type Manrope. Accent #C9A961. Body text #F5F3EF, secondary #8A9099.
Deep and calm, with a lot of empty space. A dark vignette around the edge of
every camera view. No gradients on buttons, no coloured icon circles, no bars
or stripes across the screen.

Platform: mobile, 9:16 portrait, 1080 by 1920.
